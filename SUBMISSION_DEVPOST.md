## 📌 Project Title & Tagline
* **Project Name:** Honest 4.0
* **Elevator Tagline:** Generative AI Visual Reality & Multimodal Vision Auditor for E-Commerce

---

## 💡 Inspiration
Over **$218 Billion** in e-commerce purchases are returned annually because studio marketing photos use high-key 5600K softbox diffusers, rear garment pins, and saturation filters that disguise thin fabrics, dull colors, and loose stitching. Shoppers buy what they see on screen, but receive something completely different at their door.

We built **Honest 4.0** to bring visual truth back to online shopping by bridging the gap between studio marketing fiction and real-world consumer delivery.

---

## 🍌 What It Does
Honest 4.0 is a Manifest V3 browser extension and multimodal reality engine that operates directly on Amazon, Noon, and Shein:

1. **Catalog vs. Real-World Vision Forensics:** Uses **Gemini 1.5 Flash** structured outputs to forensically compare studio listing images against authentic TikTok and YouTube unboxing video keyframes (evaluating color temperature wash, fabric drape & thickness, and mannequin pinning).
2. **Generative "Real Look" Synthesis:** Synthesizes high-fidelity, unretouched consumer images via **FLUX.1 Schnell / SD-XL** across natural daylight (5500K), office fluorescent (4500K), and warm domestic (3000K) lighting.
3. **Interactive Before/After Comparison Slider:** Allows consumers to drag a divider between the advertised promise and synthesized reality inside an encapsulated Shadow DOM.
4. **Client-Side Canvas Scorecard:** Renders a 1200×800 PNG audit badge directly in the browser with dual comparison cards, progress metrics, and one-click download.
5. **Full Bilingual Support:** Instant toggle between English and Arabic (`EN / عربي`) with RTL layout adaptation.

---

## 🛠️ How We Built It
* **Frontend & Extension:** Chrome Manifest V3, Shadow DOM (zero host style contamination), HTML5 Canvas API for lossless PNG generation, and dynamic SPA mutation observers (`History` API + `MutationObserver`).
* **Backend & AI Services:** Node.js + Python (FastAPI), Google GenAI SDK (Gemini 1.5 Flash) with Pydantic JSON schemas, and Replicate API (FLUX.1 Schnell).
* **Reliability & Resilience:** Full autonomous heuristic fallback for zero-latency, 100% offline hackathon demo stability (`test-preview.html`).
